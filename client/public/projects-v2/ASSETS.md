## Geographic animation and technical overlays

`hero-technical-overlay.svg`, `featured-rover-overlay.svg`, `india-network-overlay.svg`, and `final-earth-network.svg` are original textless vector overlays. Readable callouts stay in HTML. `india-silhouette.svg` is the vector master for the map.

`projects-india-network.webp` is a transparent 680×720, 60-frame, 10-second loop. The geographic silhouette stays fixed: blue nodes emerge at 0–2 seconds, connections brighten at 2–4 seconds, orange nodes pulse independently at 4–6 seconds, blue lights twinkle at 6–8 seconds, and three signals travel at 8–10 seconds before the loop settles back. `projects-india-poster.webp` is the static reduced-motion and offscreen fallback. Network nodes are decorative, not locations of actual schools.

India geometry uses Natural Earth's public-domain India point-of-view country boundaries: [source GeoJSON](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_10m_admin_0_countries_ind.geojson), [dataset documentation](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/). No existing repository map artwork was used.

Rebuild these assets with Python and Pillow after downloading that source file:

```sh
python scripts/generate-projects-graphics.py /path/to/ne_10m_admin_0_countries_ind.geojson
```

Motion is CSS/SVG plus intersection observers, with no added animation dependency. Continuous motion pauses offscreen and when the document is hidden. Reduced motion disables line drawing, counters, scanner, pulses, signals, and orbit rotation.
