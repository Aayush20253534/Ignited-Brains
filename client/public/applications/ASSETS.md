# Application hero imagery

These page-specific photographs were reconstructed from the supplied approved
Student and Organisation mockups with the built-in image editor. All navigation,
forms and overlaid lettering were removed. The pages render real HTML copy and
controls over the photographs. Existing website imagery remains unchanged.

Both exports are 2172 × 724, WebP quality 88.

the girl pointing upward and boy wearing glasses, uniforms, navy space learning

Use a panoramic 3:1 photograph with dark negative space on the left and complete
heads/pointing arm on the right. No UI, text, logos or watermarks.
