# Administrator workspace backgrounds

`signed-out-workspace.webp` is a clean reconstruction of the photographic left
panel in the supplied signed-out design. The supplied reference was a complete
UI screenshot, rather than a separate background asset. Its text, logo, icons
and confirmation card were removed so the screen can render accessible HTML
copy at every viewport size. Export: 1122 × 1402, WebP quality 88.

## Sign-in workspace

`client/public/admin/sign-in-workspace.webp` was generated with the built-in
image-generation tool for the administrator sign-in screen. Export: 1086 × 1448,
WebP quality 86. The background contains no embedded interface text. Branding,
security graphics, feature labels and authentication controls are rendered in HTML,
CSS and the existing SVG icon system. The original signed-out photo is retained.

Final generation prompt:

> Asset type: photographic backdrop for the Ignited Brains administrator sign-in page, an Indian STEM education organisation with navy blue and orange branding.
> Primary request: a polished, realistic STEM education team workspace with a laptop, a small educational robotics kit, an understated globe, a notebook and a warm desk lamp.
> Scene/backdrop: contemporary dark navy office and learning lab, no people. Calm, professional and welcoming rather than futuristic.
> Composition/framing: portrait 3:4 photograph. The laptop, modest robotics kit and warm lamp occupy the lower right third. Eye-level desk perspective, realistic proportions, shallow depth of field. Preserve enough of the room that it looks like a natural photographic scene.
> Lighting/mood: soft navy-blue ambient light with a restrained warm orange desk-lamp glow, tactile realistic wood and metal textures.
> Constraints: no lettering, logos, signage, emblems, readable UI, people or watermarks. Laptop screen has an abstract dark blue background with no text. No NASA or other space-agency branding. Avoid excessive holograms, glowing neon, busy details, floating UI cards and oversaturated orange.
